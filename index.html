About Saapasá

A cozy café in Whitefield, Bengaluru

Named after the soulful notes of a musical scale, Saapasá Café sits on Vijayanagar Main Road, opposite NVT Life Square — a warm, softly lit room of backlit arches, dried wheat and cane, rated 4.7★ on Google by our neighbours. Everything is made in-house: soft wheat focaccia, hand-tossed pizzas, slow-simmered pasta sauces, freshly made muesli and smoothie bowls, and Andhra comfort classics like avakaya and gongura rice bowls. Whether it's specialty coffee at sunrise, a long breakfast, or dessert after dark, we're your neighbourhood café in Whitefield — come find your note.
